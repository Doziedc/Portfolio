About Me

I got into design through digital art and a love for creating things from scratch. My journey started with Canva, then grew into Blender, where I picked up a bit of 3D animation. From there, I taught myself UI design, and after a while, I started learning front-end development too.

What drives me is curiosity. I don't just want to know how something works, I want to experiment with it, break it, rebuild it, and eventually make something of my own. That mindset is what took me from being a digital artist to becoming a UI/UX designer and front-end developer.
